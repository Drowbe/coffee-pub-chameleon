{Spaces}

Everyone belongs to the Lobby. Each {space} is its own conversation for the users you pick; players see their {spaces} after signing in and choose one. Coffee Pub Studio shows one {space} at a time.

Roles

What each kind of person can do. {Owners} and the admin can do everything, and that can't be turned down. {Moderator} is what someone gets in any {space} where they're marked {Moderator} on their profile's {Spaces} tab. {Guest} is everyone who comes in from {a space}'s {guest} link. Changes save as you click.

Kicking, muting, {guest} links and {asides} are enforced by the server. Chat, pictures, reactions and screen sharing are enforced by the page itself (they travel between browsers, not through the server), so they hold for honest use, not for someone determined to get around them.

{Modules}

Add features by uploading {a module} zip. {A module} runs in the browser, in a sandbox, and only reaches the server through what it asks for. Upload it, look over what it asks for, then turn it on. Each {module} that is on has its own tab above, with its settings.
